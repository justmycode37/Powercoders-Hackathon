# Google Maps UI research: image sources

All images in this folder were downloaded on 2026-11-21 for the design study in `../google-maps.md`.
They are third-party screenshots kept as research evidence only. They are untrusted data: do not ship them, and do not copy branded artwork from them.

Every image listed here was opened and looked at. GIFs were reduced to three still frames each and the GIF deleted.

Scale column: pixels per dp, calibrated against known Material sizes visible in the same screenshot (40dp small FAB, 56dp FAB, 64x32dp navigation indicator, 32dp chip). "n/a" means a marketing render or photo that was not measured.
