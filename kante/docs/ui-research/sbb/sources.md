# SBB Mobile research – image and source index

Collected 2026-11-21. Every image below was opened and looked at (alone or in a
contact sheet) before it was used in `../sbb.md`. Images are third-party material kept
for internal design research only; do not ship them.

"App version" is what the source shows or its date implies. The app's row and card
anatomy was the same in every source from 2022 to 2026, so older frames are still
used for measurement; where versions differ, `../sbb.md` says so.
